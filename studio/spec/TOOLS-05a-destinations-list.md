## Platform descriptions

### OK.ru (`ok`)

Odnoklassniki, OK.ru, is a social network service for classmates and old friends. It is popular in the Russian Federation and former Soviet Republics.

### VK Live (`vk`)

VK.com is a famous social network in Russia and eastern Europe that supports live streaming with VK Live. The platform's live streaming tool allows you to share your life and experiences in real time to thousands of people around the world. VK Live also supports an Android application.

### Facebook (`facebook`)

Facebook, the world's largest social network, has entered the live streaming game. Facebook Live boasts the largest audience globally, great interactive features for audience members, and filters for customizing live streams. The streaming content on Facebook is as diverse as the platform itself; publishers include everyone from average people to world-famous celebrities. <p> <a href="https://support.restream.io/en/articles/2251873-how-to-connect-facebook-to-restream" target="_blank">Learn "How to connect Facebook" here. </a> </p>
